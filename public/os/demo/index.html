Demo controls

Open the panel: tap the clock three times quickly (within 1.5 seconds), or press Shift+D. In the panel, press 1 to 4, or tap the button. Esc closes it. The panel closes itself before a control runs.

The four controls

KeyControlWhat it doesWhen
1ResetEvery app back to its demo data, the board back to day 1, the first-open sentence armed again. Says "Reset. Day 1."Before going on, and between rehearsals.
2Next morningMoves the seeded week on a day. Murmur proposes "Put the bus first?"; answer Yes and the tile says UP.1:35, "It learns, with permission".
3ModeSwitches who speaks: Murmur, or the screen reader (Murmur goes silent and writes to the status line). Says which.Race in Murmur mode; switch for the 10 s TalkBack beat, then back.

Before going on

  1. Open the panel, press 1 Reset. You hear "Reset. Day 1."
  2. Reload the phone page. You hear "Tap anywhere and tell me what you need." once.
  3. Reset leaves the mode on Murmur, and every row of Your look at its default.
